# UI provenance

The controls are an original C/RGB565 adaptation to the XDJ-XZ's 800x480 screen.
`font.h` contains original minimal 5x7 uppercase/number glyphs and needs no
external font files.

Interaction requirements were checked against the local upstream snapshot
`e74e199603e2a25567950ca72997c38d17ada4e8`:

- `docs/stems.md`: three faders, held name mute, bypass and modification dot.
- `docs/groove-circuit.md`: first bank A-H, stem colors, loaded/playing states,
  unassigned hot cues and closing-row persistence.
- `docs/xpad.md`: six lengths, vertical pitch, HOLD, volume, event overdub and
  closing-panel ownership release.
- `docs/mod-settings.md` and `docs/themes.md`: default-off switches, server
  location/address and ORIGINAL plus six alternative themes.

CYBERPUNK, NEON, MOCHA, AURORA and SANDSTONE use the upstream `ground`, `ink`,
`alarm` and stem seed values from `package/deck/mods/theme/presets.c` (MIT OR
Apache-2.0). ORIGINAL and WHITE use locally chosen legible control colors.
This is control-palette support; it does not reproduce the upstream stock
JUCE image/gradient transforms or claim full native-screen theme parity.
Upstream licenses are bundled at `../audio/licenses/` in this port.

Screenshots generated by `test_ui` are clearly labeled host demonstrations.
They are not photographs or captures of functioning XZ audio features.
